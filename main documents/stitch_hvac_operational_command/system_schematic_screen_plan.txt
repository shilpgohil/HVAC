Screen ID: SYSTEM_GRAPHIC_LIVE
Name: Live System Schematic
Primary user: Control Room Operator
Purpose: Provide a real-time, visually-oriented representation of the physical HVAC system (Chiller, Air Handling Unit, and Room context) with live telemetry overlays.
Entry points: Global Navigation (System Map), Operational Overview drill-down.
Primary task: Monitor physical flow and mechanical state.
Primary action: Hover/Select equipment for quick-stats.
Secondary actions: Navigate to Equipment Detail, Acknowledge localized alarms.
Critical information: Chilled water flow/temp, air duct pressures, fan status, room temperature/CO2.
Secondary information: Damper positions, pump speeds.
Required data: Real-time telemetry for all points identified in the reference diagram (Sensors T, H, DP, P, F, CO2).
Important states: Normal, Fault (component-level highlighting), Offline (stale data styling).
Permission constraints: View-only for general operators; control overlays for senior engineers.
Responsive behavior: Scale schematic for tablet; scrollable viewport for mobile with sticky KPI summary.
Related screens: Operational Overview, Equipment Detail (Chiller/AHU).
